PadelSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

PadelSync is a padel and tennis scoring app for phones and watches, published by Charbel Rizk (CRDevTool). This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • PadelSync has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. Nothing you do in it is sent to us or to any server.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Scores are shared only with the other devices on your court, directly over Bluetooth.

Information kept on your device

The app stores the following on your own device, so that it works between sessions:

  • your matches: the score, the match format and the match history;
  • player names, if you type any;
  • the name shown for your device or court;
  • your settings;
  • a random identifier the app creates on first launch, used only so the devices on a court can tell each other apart. It is not linked to your identity, your phone number or any account.

We cannot see any of this. You can erase it at any time by deleting the app or clearing its data.

Information shared with nearby devices

When you host or join a court, your device exchanges the following with the other devices on that court, directly over Bluetooth:

  • the match score and format;
  • player names, if any were entered;
  • your device's name and its random identifier.

While you host a court, its name is visible to nearby devices that are looking for courts. Joining a court needs the code shown on the host's screen. None of this information leaves the court or reaches us.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Bluetooth: to find, host and join courts.
  • Location, on Android 11 and earlier only: those Android versions require it before an app may look for Bluetooth devices. PadelSync does not read, use or store your location.
  • Notifications: to show that a court is open on your device.
  • Vibration: to signal a point scored by another player.

The spoken score uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. The app does not use the microphone and records nothing.

Purchases

If you buy a paid feature, the purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether the paid feature is active on your account.

Crash reports and testing

Apple and Google may share crash reports and diagnostics with us if you have agreed to share them with developers in your device settings. These reports do not identify you. If you take part in a test version through TestFlight or Google Play testing, feedback you choose to send reaches us through those services.

Children

PadelSync is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
